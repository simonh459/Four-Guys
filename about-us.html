About Our Restaurant...

Four Guys is a modern restaurant specialising in pizza and burgers located in the heart of Belfast. Established in 2022, Four Guys focuses on delivering high-quality food in a relaxing environment with an emphasis on customer-service. Since opening, Four Guys has became a popular local spot for local and tourist alike, combining friendly customer service and great food.

The owners of Four Guys thought long and hard what makes a great restaurant. The food only makes a part of the restaurant, but the customers are what makes a restaurant complete. Our customer service policy ensures that the customer must enjoy every minute within our restaurant. This ensures that we create a welcoming and warm environment along with exciting and tasteful dishes for all to try.

Whilst we have a great focus on the customer, we still spend many hours perfecting and tweaking our menu. Four Guys attempts to get people to step outside their food-comfort zones, trying exciting and new flavours. Many foods are rotated in and out of our menu as we listen carefully to customer ratings on how to improve our menu.

At Four Guys, we pride ourselves with being among the highest quality restaurants in Northern Ireland. Have a quick look at our favourite awards!

Customer First Approach

As a restaurant, we put our visitors at the centre of everything we do. From the moment you walk through the door , our focus is on creating a welcoming and comforting environment where you feel valued. We prioritise attentive service and aim to respond quickly to consumer queries to ensure an enjoyable dining experience.

High Quality Food

We are comitted to serving delicious food made from fresh, carefully selected ingredients. We specialise in creating flavourful pizzas and tasty burgers, looking to deliver a consistent quality with each bite. Our focus is combining great taste with great prices to create satisfying meals which leaves everyone happy.
