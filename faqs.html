FAQs

This page contains some of the more commonly asked questions we receive, which will hopefully be enough to answer any queries you may have. If the answer to your question cannot be found here, please feel free to reach out to us whether through our Feedback form or coming in person and asking our staff.        

We are a modern restaurant specialising in both burgers and pizza that is located in the heart of Belfast. We were established in 2022 and focus on delivering high-quality food in a relaxing environment, placing emphasis on our customer service in order to ensure satisfaction. Our doors are open to both locals and tourists alike with the sole goal of delivering a dining experience that captures the heart and soul of Belfast.

Our Menu indicates vegetarian and vegan dishes. Some of our Pizzas can also be made can also be made with non-gluten containing ingredients. Please inform our staff of your dietary needs before placing your order.

As a company we recognise the importance of the safe management of allergens and the serious effect they can have on certain individuals. We have strict policies in place to deal with handling allergens, as well as a strict auditing process to ensure that these policies are fully complied with in all of our restaurants.

However, our suppliers and kitchens handle numerous ingredients and allergens. Whilst we have strict controls in place to reduce the risk of contamination, unfortunately it is not possible for us to guarantee that our dishes will be 100% allergen free.

Please speak to the restaurant manager if you have any food allergies prior to placing your order so we can take extra care preparing your dish.
